— The Truth

Golf Has A Gatekeeping Problem.

For over a century, golf has been built around a very specific kind of person. If you're not that person, the path in can feel cold. Expensive. Watched. Or just plain unwelcoming. MySwing and Me exists because golf belongs to everyone — but the on-ramp shouldn't require you to fit a mold.

$150 lessons. 2-hour blocks.

Most golfers can't justify the time or money for a single lesson — let alone the dozens it takes to build a real swing.

Ranges feel watched and judgmental.

Beginners get hovered over. Bad swings get glances. The whole environment is built for people who already know what they're doing.

Women get talked over, condescended to, and "corrected" by strangers.

Walking into a pro shop or onto a range as a woman new to golf can feel like crashing a party where no one wanted you. MySwing and Me eliminates that entirely.

Country club culture is alive and well.

Golf has too often been treated like it belongs to one kind of person — with one kind of last name, from one kind of neighborhood. We disagree.

— Questions

Real Answers To Real Questions.

I've literally never played golf. Will this work? +

Yes — that's exactly who MySwing and Me is built for. We start from the absolute foundation: how to hold the club, how to stand, where the ball goes. You don't need to know anything coming in. We assume nothing and explain everything.

I'm just a regular person. Is this really for someone like me? +

MySwing and Me was built specifically for the everyday Joe, Jamal, and Jorge — and Jasmine, Maria, and everyone else who doesn't fit the country club mold. You work, you have a life, you don't have golf money. You're our people.

I'm a woman new to golf and tired of being the only one. Does this actually help? +

This is one of our flagship audiences. You can build an entire swing privately — in your home, garage, or yard — and never set foot on a range until you feel ready. The AI coach speaks to you directly and respectfully. No mansplaining. No hand-on-hip "corrections." Just real coaching that treats you like the adult you are.

Will the AI coach be condescending? +

No. We've trained the AI coach to be direct, clear, and respectful — never patronizing. It explains what's happening in your swing without dumbing things down or talking down to you. You can also pick your coaching style: Mentor (warm, explanatory), Grinder (intense, direct), or Competitor (data-driven).

Do I need special camera equipment? +

No. Any phone made in the last 5 years works. Prop it up on a chair, lean it against your golf bag, or grab a $20 phone tripod. The app includes built-in alignment guides so every recording is consistent.

Can I really learn without a real coach? +

For 95% of golfers — yes, completely. MySwing and Me replaces the diagnosis, correction, and plan that lessons provide. If you eventually want to work with a real pro, great — you can log those lessons and the AI coach will align with what your pro teaches. We're a supplement, not a replacement when you do want a human coach.

What if I don't have access to a range? +

You don't need one. Practice swings at home are how you build mechanics. MySwing and Me is built specifically for backyard, basement, garage, driveway, and living room practice. When you eventually want to hit balls, even a local park or empty field works.

How is this different from YouTube videos? +

YouTube gives you generic tips. MySwing and Me looks at YOUR swing and tells you what YOU specifically need to fix. It remembers your baseline. It catches when old habits return. It builds a plan for your body and your faults. YouTube is a library — MySwing and Me is a coach.

Will the AI judge my swing? +

It diagnoses — it doesn't judge. There's no "good" or "bad" — just observations, root causes, and corrections. You're the only one who sees your swings. No leaderboards. No comparisons. No public anything.

I don't fit the "golfer" stereotype. Will I feel out of place using this? +

You'll feel right at home. Every word, every image, and every coaching choice in MySwing and Me was built around the assumption that golf belongs to everyone — and that the path in shouldn't require you to fit anyone's mold.

What about club fitting for women — without the "shrink it and pink it" garbage? +

Real fit recommendations based on your height, swing speed, tempo, and body mechanics — not based on aesthetics or assumptions. Women's recommendations include specific brands that actually design for female biomechanics, not just lighter pink versions of men's clubs.

What if I want to work with a real coach later? +

Perfect. MySwing and Me is great preparation — you'll show up to your first lesson with a baseline, a foundation, and the vocabulary to actually understand what your coach is teaching. And our Pro Lessons feature lets you log lessons so the AI stays in sync with your real-world coach.
